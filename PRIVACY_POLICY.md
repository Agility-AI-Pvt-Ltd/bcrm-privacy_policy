## 3. WhatsApp and Meta Integration

Our Service integrates with Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform.

Businesses may connect their WhatsApp Business Accounts to our Service using Meta's authorization and onboarding mechanisms, including WhatsApp Embedded Signup where available.

When a business chooses to connect WhatsApp:

1. The business is directed through Meta's authorization experience.
2. The business authorizes the requested access.
3. Meta provides our application with information necessary to establish the integration.
4. Our Service associates the connected WhatsApp Business Account with the business's organization within our platform.
5. Our Service uses the authorized information to provide WhatsApp messaging and related functionality.

We do not request or require a business customer to provide its Meta password directly to us.

We process WhatsApp-related information only to provide the functionality authorized by the business and for legitimate operational, security, and legal purposes.

Meta may separately collect and process information according to its own policies.

For more information about Meta's privacy practices, please review:

https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/
